# Design reference (donaisa.com, captured 2026-10-22)

Visual reference only. None of the original's text, images, videos or links go into this site.

## Platform
Wix (Thunderbolt). Home is a one-page layout with in-page anchors; separate pages: `/filmmaker`, `/galeria`, `/contact-9`.

## Tokens
| Role | Original | Our substitute (Google Fonts) |
|---|---|---|
| Logo | Image, condensed heavy grotesque, all caps | Anton |
| Section titles | Lulo Clean One Bold, caps, tight tracking (-0.1em), 30–45px | Unbounded 700, letter-spacing -0.04em |
| Nav | Avenir 85 Heavy 16px, lowercase, #594E48 | Nunito Sans 600 |
| Bio body | Bitter 15px | Bitter |

| Card titles | Poppins SemiBold 13px | Poppins 600 |
| Card tags | Quicksand 10px | Quicksand |

| Video titles | Poppins ExtraLight/700 caps 14px | Poppins 600 |

Colors: text #000, nav #594E48, bg #FFF, bio band #F4F4F4, director band #FFF6FE, brand chip ~#F9DDF5, footer band ~#ECEAE3.

## Layout (desktop, ~980px content column)
1. Sticky white header: logo left; nav filmmaker / creator / fotógrafa / diretora; right: sobre, contato, IG + TikTok icons.
2. Hero: horizontal carousel of tall 3:4 portrait photos, 4.5 visible, arrow at right.
3. Sobre: grey band; handwritten name heading, two paragraphs left, framed portrait right.
4. Filmmaker: centered title; 5 vertical 9:16 video cards with play icon; title + tag under each; pill "ver mais" button.
5. Galeria: title + subtitle left, 4×2 photo grid right with soft shadow; "VER MAIS" pill.
6. Creator: title, handle + tagline, social icons; two embedded profile cards (Instagram/TikTok).
7. Campanhas: 3-column grid of 4:5 images with brand label chip overlapping bottom.
8. Diretora: pink band, title right-aligned + subtitle; 3-col grid of 16:9 thumbnails, each with title and YouTube "assistir agora".
9. Full-bleed autoplay video banner; thin footer.

Subpages: /filmmaker = 5-col grid of all video cards; /galeria = 4-col masonry; /contato = heading "Contato" + email/phone rows with icons.

Motion: Wix fade/slide-in on scroll; hover zoom on thumbnails.
